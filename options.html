yt-dlp Link Launcher

Clicking a link to a video on any site yt-dlp supports opens it in your local program. Hold Alt while clicking to open a link normally.

{url} URL-encoded link, {rawurl} link as-is, {id} YouTube video ID (empty on other sites).
Example: ytplayer://open?url={url}

Subdomains are included, so reddit.com also covers old.reddit.com.
